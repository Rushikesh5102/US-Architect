Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

At US Architects, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or contact us through our contact form.

Information We Collect

Contact Form Information

When you use our contact form, we collect the following information:

  • Name: Your full name as provided
  • Email Address: Your email address for communication purposes
  • Phone Number: To contact you regarding your inquiry
  • Message: The content of your inquiry or message
  • Timestamp: The date and time when you submitted the form

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through our contact form for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide architectural consultation
  • To communicate with you about our design services
  • To maintain internal records for project management

Data Storage and Security

Your contact form submissions are securely stored using Google Apps Script integration. This data is:

  • Stored in a secure Google Sheet accessible only to authorized US Architects personnel
  • Protected by Google's industry-leading security measures
  • Used solely for responding to your construction or interior design inquiry
